Compare new construction near Lexington using the written specifications and lot details. A model home is a useful tour, but it isn’t automatically the house or the contract you’re buying.

## What should you confirm about the home and lot?

Confirm the included finishes, options, lot, service setup, and any restrictions in writing. Ask which features in the model are upgrades. Review the site and route separately from the floor plan.

## How should you compare an advertised incentive?

Compare the written incentive with its eligibility conditions and your full financing and purchase costs. Ask your lender to evaluate available choices. Don’t compare headline incentives while ignoring rate, fees, or required providers.

## Does new construction need inspection planning?

Discuss independent inspections and access at appropriate stages before signing. Read the builder’s contract and coordinate dates with the builder. New materials do not answer every question about installation or the site.

## What should you ask about timing and warranties?

Ask how the agreement handles delays, completion standards, walkthroughs, warranty coverage, and service requests. Have an attorney review legal questions. Keep important representations in the written agreement.

## What to request from a builder before signing

Ask for a written specification sheet, allowance schedule, site plan, anticipated completion provisions, warranty coverage, and rules for change orders. Confirm whether landscaping, appliances, utility connections, and driveway work are included. If the builder offers a rate incentive, compare total loan costs and lender terms instead of looking only at the advertised rate. Discuss independent inspections at appropriate construction milestones with your agent and inspector.
