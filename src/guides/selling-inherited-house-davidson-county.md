For an inherited house in Davidson County, the first question is who has the legal authority to sell. Get that resolved before repairs, promises to buyers, or a listing schedule.

## Who needs to confirm authority to sell?

A North Carolina attorney should review ownership, estate documents, and authority to transfer the property. Ask who must sign and what needs to happen first. A family understanding is not a substitute for title review.

## What records should you gather?

Gather available deed information, estate documents, tax records, mortgage information, insurance details, and repair history. Keep sensitive documents in a secure channel rather than a general website form. Ask the attorney which records matter for your situation.

## How do you handle cleanout and repairs?

Agree on authority and important belongings before starting the cleanout. Get written estimates and compare a listing in current condition with targeted preparation or an as-is offer. Avoid spending before you know the sale path.

## How do you build a realistic timeline?

Base the timeline on legal readiness, property work, market preparation, and closing requirements. Ask which steps can happen together and which depend on an earlier decision. Don’t promise a date before the dependencies are clear.
