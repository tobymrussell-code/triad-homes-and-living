Compare Wallburg and Welcome through the property and the routine you’ll run from it. More space can be useful, but only after you know what that space requires.

## How should you compare the locations?

Compare actual addresses in Wallburg and Welcome against the same regular destinations. Test your work route and normal errands. Don’t use the center of either area as a substitute for the property.

## What should you verify about services?

Verify water, sewer or well and septic, electricity, and internet directly for the parcel. Ask about connections, permits, and available history. Nearby service doesn’t establish service at the house.

## What matters when you want land or an outbuilding?

Check boundaries, legal access, restrictions, and permitted use before planning an outbuilding or addition. Get the relevant records reviewed. A visible driveway or open field is not enough to establish the rights you need.

## How do you weigh extra space against upkeep?

Estimate the work and costs that come with the lot, systems, and structures. Walk the property and ask for written estimates when needed. Decide whether the extra responsibilities match how you want to spend your time.
